# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in XIYO projects a harassment-free experience for everyone and to act in ways that support an open, welcoming, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for different viewpoints, constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on the health of the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that conflict with these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to [bunny@xiyo.dev](mailto:bunny@xiyo.dev). Reports will be reviewed fairly and the reporter's privacy will be respected.
